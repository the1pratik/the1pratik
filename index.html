Profile

A litigation lawyer, on your side of the table

I am a commercial disputes lawyer at Karanjawala & Co., New Delhi — one of India's leading litigation firms — and independent legal counsel to agencies on monthly retainer.

The retainer practice exists because growing agencies sign more contracts in a month than most companies do in a year, yet almost none have a lawyer on call. I fill that gap: fixed fee, fast turnaround, plain-English advice.

My current retainer clients span digital marketing, online reputation management, event management and online community management — so the legal patterns of agency work are ones I see every week, not once a year.

The Problem

Agencies run on contracts.
Almost none have counsel.

01

A contract stack per campaign

Client MSAs, influencer deals, vendor POs, venue bookings, talent releases — every new project multiplies the paperwork.

02

Law-firm economics punish questions

Per-matter billing means every query costs money. So contracts get signed unread — until one of them breaks.

03

One dispute erases a quarter

Unpaid invoices, IP ownership fights, ASCI notices, exclusivity breaches — expensive precisely because nobody read the paper early.

A retainer flips the economics: fixed monthly fee, every contract read, disputes caught while they're still cheap.
